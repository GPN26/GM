# Source notes for the first 100 (12 November 2026)

This is a personal directory of original historical writing, not a republication of articles. Newspaper-like cards are newly typeset from headline and publication metadata. No third-party photographs, scans or complete article texts are included in the site. Links open the source publisher's page. Publishers retain copyright; a working link does not grant reproduction rights.

## Selecting the featured link

1. Direct reporting of the grandmaster milestone, ideally published at the time.
2. Contemporary chess reporting explicitly documenting the final qualification or conditional approval, **labelled accurately**.
3. When no such item is located: a near-period player-focused profile confirming when the player became a GM.
4. FIDE records and supporting interviews remain in **Other articles & sources** rather than being preferred over a readable news report.

## Important residual uncertainty

The first GM newspaper declarations remain unlocated for some players. In particular, #3 Thipsay, #4 Kunte, #5 Sasikiran, #10 R. B. Ramesh, #13 Deepan Chakkravarthy, #16 G. N. Gopal, #17 Abhijeet Gupta, #18 Arun Prasad, #19 Kidambi and #39 Ashwin Jayaram have featured sources that are **not unqualified, verified first announcements**. See the CSV audit for every entry.

#9 Sandipan Chanda's November 2003 Rediff item is a report of **conditional** FIDE approval; it explicitly notes the 2500 requirement. #31 Shyam Sundar's Dec 2012 contemporaneous announcement discusses reaching the required live rating, though numbered rosters often use 2013. #1 Anand's 1987 GM breakthrough is documented in a Jan 1988 historical magazine.

## Link availability

Links were selected by examining indexed source content where accessible. Some sites may geo-block, paywall or change pages, and not all external URLs were independently tested by a real browser session. The CSV source audit is a research index, **not** a claim that every third-party URL was live-tested.

## Editing

Update `data/grandmasters.js` when a better source is found. Put the old featured source into that player's `more` list. Source `kind` values: `announcement`, `contemporary`, `confirmation`, `norm`, `record`, `clipping`.
